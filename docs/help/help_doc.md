# Help - How to Use 24 Solver?

**24 Solver** is a calculator designed to solve the **"Make 24" puzzle** -- but it’s capable of much more. Given enough time, it can find every possible solution to these puzzles; it can also handle "Make 10" or "Make -24" challenges.

**How to use**:
Just like a standard calculator, use the numeric keypad to enter your numbers. The numbers you input will appear in the [INPUT] display. Once you’ve entered a number correctly, press [NEXT] to store it, or short-press [DEL] to remove a mistake.

Repeat this process until all your numbers (up to 5) have been entered, then press [SOLVE] to get the results.

Press [TARGET] to change the target number, and press it again to save your choice -- this allows you to solve for any target value (e.g., "Make X"). You can also adjust your preferred target value via the settings menu in the top-right corner.

Try long-pressing [CLEAR] or [DEL] for a little surprise.

You can also disable the keypad sounds in the settings.

You can reopen this help page in the settings.
